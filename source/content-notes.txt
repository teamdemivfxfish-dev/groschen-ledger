Recipes brought up to date with the wiki (30 October): 1,259 recipes, up from 1,205.

- Steel bars are now called steel, as on the wiki and the pack cards. Any price you set for steel bars carries over.
- Linen is made from 2 flax. Padding, bandages and bowstrings now use linen, and many tailoring pieces no longer need padding.
- Flax and hemp fibre are new market goods (set worth 6 and 5).
- 41 new furniture pieces with their pictures: icons, tapestries, lanterns, candles, wreaths, pelts, hearths, the lute and more. Pieces bought only with coin show that cost.
- Second recipes for bandages, cooked hare, roast chicken leg, crayfish and trout are listed beside the first.
- Many armour pieces moved levels and changed materials, meat dishes now take two cuts, and several cooked dishes take a side.
