Trade packs: pick towns instead of typing kilometres

The pack tabs now ask where the pack is packed and where you sell it (Suchdol, Kuttenberg, Bylany, Grund, Miskowitz, Pschitoky, Raborsch). The ledger works out the distance between the two convoy masters by itself, for example Suchdol to Kuttenberg is 3.3 km.
- choose "Other place (type km)" in either box to type a distance yourself
- a pair closer than 1.5 km is flagged, since the convoy master won't take it
- the "Got a surplus?" box uses the same towns
