KCD Roleplay · The crafts reckoned

The Groschen Ledger

Set what goods fetch at the auction house, and the ledger reckons what every one of the realm's 1,205 crafts costs to make, bench fees and all.

Opening the ledger…

Cost to make one = (ingredients + bench fee) ÷ pieces per run. Parts such as steel bars, leather and hafts are reckoned from their cheapest recipe, or from a buy price set under Market prices › Parts when that is cheaper. Colour and coat-of-arms variants can cost a little more.

Recipes from wiki.kcdroleplay.com, starting prices from its gathering and merchant pages (23 Oct 2026). Art and icons from Kingdom Come: Deliverance II by Warhorse Studios. A fan-made tool, not affiliated with KCD Roleplay or KCD:MP.
